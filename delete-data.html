Delete your data

For Hopaboo: Bunny Runner, Hopaboo 3D: Bunny Runner and Lampo · Developer: Ova Corporation

In shortSend us an email with your in‑game nickname and the game name. We delete your online data within 30 days. No account or password is needed.

How to request deletion

  • Step 1. Open the game and note your nickname (shown on the leaderboard and in Settings).
  • Step 2. Email robert.ovadiuc2@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, your nickname and the game name (Hopaboo: Bunny Runner, Hopaboo 3D: Bunny Runner or Lampo).
  • Step 3. We confirm by email once your data has been deleted, within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your random player ID and nickname
  • Your scores on the online leaderboards
  • Your friends list and race history

What is kept

  • We keep nothing else about you. We do not hold your name, email (except the deletion request itself), location or payment details.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own rules; we cannot delete them.
  • Ad data is handled by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Delete the data on your device

Your progress (coins, items, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data, to delete it. In the game you can also use Settings → “New player (guest)” to start over with a new player ID.
