Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Developer: Ova Corporation

In shortOur games do not ask for your real name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your progress is saved on your device. Online features (leaderboards, friends and races) use a random player ID and the nickname you choose. Ads and purchases are handled by Google.

Who we are

This policy covers the mobile games published by Ova Corporation ("we", "us"):

  • Hopaboo: Bunny Runner (com.ovacorporation.hopaboo)
  • Hopaboo 3D: Bunny Runner (com.ovacorporation.hopaboo3d)

In this policy, "the game" means either of these games. It explains what information the games use, why, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your phone or tablet: coins, items, upgrades, missions, challenges, settings, language and scores. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Online leaderboards

To show the online leaderboards, the game uses Talo (trytalo.com), a game backend service. When you play online, it sends:

  • a random player ID created by the game (not linked to your name, phone number or email);
  • the nickname you choose;
  • your scores.

Your nickname and scores are visible to other players. Please do not use your real full name as a nickname. Nicknames are checked by a filter on your device to block offensive words. Like any online service, Talo's servers receive your device's IP address when the game connects. See Talo's privacy policy.

Friends and races

If you add friends or join a race, the game uses Talo to store your friend connections (player IDs and nicknames) and to exchange race information in a private room of up to 5 players, such as the race track seed, your progress and your result. Only players in that room see this information. You can play without using these features.

Google Play Games (Hopaboo 3D)

Hopaboo 3D may sign you in automatically to Google Play Games Services if you use it on your device. Google provides this service under its own terms; we do not receive your email address or password. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob: you choose to watch one, for example to continue a run or to get a bonus. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In‑app purchases

Optional items (such as potions) can be bought through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought, so the game can deliver it.

Notifications

The game may show reminder notifications (for example, that your daily gift is ready) if you allow notifications. They are scheduled on your device and do not send any data to us. You can turn them off in your device settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without using leaderboards, friends or races.
  • You can change your nickname in the game, or start again as a new guest player.
  • You can ask us to delete your online data (player ID, nickname, scores and friends) by emailing us with your nickname and the game name.
  • If you live in the EU, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion of your data, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Data security and retention

Data sent online travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Online data is kept while the game's online features are active, or until you ask us to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the games change. The dates at the top show the latest version.
